Beginner tips before you start

  • Hold the pen loosely and move your whole arm, not just your fingers. Long lines come out smoother.
  • Draw each line in one quick, confident stroke. Slow, shaky lines are harder to keep on the guide.
  • Messed up? Press Undo (or Ctrl+Z) and go again. Every artist redraws lines.
  • Use a stylus for pressure-sensitive lines: press harder for thicker strokes.
