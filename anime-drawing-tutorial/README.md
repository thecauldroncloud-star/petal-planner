# Anime Tracing Dojo

A beginner anime drawing tutorial where you learn by tracing. Each lesson breaks a drawing into small steps. Trace the pink guide line with a finger, mouse or stylus, and a "trace match" score shows how closely you followed it.

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. There is nothing to install or build.

## Lessons

1. **Warm-up Lines**: straight lines, zig-zags, S-curves and circles
2. **The Anime Head**: skull circle, guide cross, jaw, ears, neck
3. **Sparkly Anime Eyes**: brows, lash line, lower lid, iris, pupil and highlights
4. **Nose, Mouth & Blush**
5. **Anime Hair**: volume, spiky bangs, side locks, back hair, ahoge
6. **Happy Expression**: closed eyes, open smile, sparkles
7. **Chibi Character**: a full chibi from head to shoes

## Features

- **Watch the stroke** animates each guide line so you can see where it starts and which way it goes. A purple dot marks every starting point.
- **Trace match score** compares your lines to the guide. Finishing a lesson gives 1 to 3 stars, and your best result is saved in the browser.
- **Draw from memory** repeats a lesson with the guide hidden and still scores you.
- Pressure-sensitive lines with a stylus (Apple Pencil, Wacom and similar), plus palm rejection once a pen is detected.
- Undo (Ctrl+Z), clear step, pen size, guide opacity, and saving your drawing as a PNG.
- Light and dark themes. Works on phones, tablets and desktop.
